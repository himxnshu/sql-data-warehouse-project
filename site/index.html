How This Enterprise Data Warehouse Works

A straightforward guide to understanding how this project solves real enterprise data problems using Microsoft SQL Server and the Medallion Architecture.

⚠️

1. The Real-World Problem

Companies don't store all their data in one place. Sales live in the CRM system, while logistics and birthdays live in the ERP system. Data formats don't match, customer records have duplicates, and values like countries are typed differently (`USA`, `US`, `United States`).

⚙️

2. The Medallion Solution

Instead of querying messy source files directly, data flows through three organized tiers:

🥉 Bronze: Ingests raw CSVs without touching them.
🥈 Silver: Cleans, deduplicates, and connects CRM with ERP.
🥇 Gold: Pre-joins everything into simple tables for business decisions.

🚀

3. Why This Matters

Executives can ask questions like "Which country brings the highest profit?" and get answers in milliseconds without freezing the operational database or calculating complex joins every single time.

🥉 BRONZE TIER Raw Staging

Immutable Landing Tables

Direct high-speed ingestion of external CRM and ERP CSV files using T-SQL BULK INSERT with TABLOCK optimization for minimal transaction logging.

  • Zero Mutation: Preserves raw data exactly as delivered.
  • Audit Reservoir: Full traceability back to original flat files.
  • Tables: crm_cust_info, crm_prd_info, crm_sales_details, erp_CUST_AZ12, erp_LOC_A101, erp_PX_CAT_G1V2.
🥈 SILVER TIER Cleansed & Integrated

Conformance & SCD2 Historization

Executes data cleansing, deduplication, composite key splitting, and Slowly Changing Dimension (SCD Type 2) tracking via silver.load_silver.

  • Deduplication: ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY cst_id ORDER BY cst_create_date DESC).
  • SCD2 Tracking: Uses LEAD(prd_start_dt) OVER (...) - 1 for active product validity dates.
  • Normalization: Strips legacy prefixes (e.g. NAS) and standardizes countries.
🥇 GOLD TIER Business Star Schema

Analytical Star Schema Views

High-performance dimensional views engineered for executive business intelligence and consumed securely by the FastAPI microservice.

  • Dimensions: gold.dim_customers, gold.dim_products.
  • Facts: gold.fact_sales (joined to dimensional surrogate keys).
  • Decoupled UI: Zero database credentials exposed to presentation clients.
